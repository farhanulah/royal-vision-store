How to Choose the Perfect Frame for Your Face Shape

The secret to great-looking eyewear is balance — here is how to find yours.

Find your face shape

Look in a mirror and trace the outline of your face. Most faces fall into one of four families: round (soft curves, similar width and length), square (strong jaw, broad forehead), oval (balanced proportions), or heart (wider forehead, narrower chin). Once you know your shape, choosing a frame becomes easy.

Round face — add angles

Rectangular and square frames add definition to a round face. Aviator styles also work beautifully. Explore our Sunglasses and Classic Frames categories for structured shapes that sharpen your look.

Square face — soften with curves

Round and oval frames balance a strong jawline. Thin metal frames keep the look light and premium — a classic MOSCOT-style silhouette is a great place to start.

Oval face — almost everything works

An oval face suits most frame shapes. The only rule: keep the frame as wide as, or slightly wider than, the broadest part of your face. Bold designs like our PRADA and MAYBACH styles were made for you.

Heart face — balance the chin

Frames that are wider at the bottom, or light-coloured and rimless styles, balance a narrower chin. Ladies' cat-eye designs also lift the look wonderfully — see the Ladies category.
